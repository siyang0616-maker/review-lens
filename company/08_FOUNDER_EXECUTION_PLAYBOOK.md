# Founder Execution Playbook

## Purpose

This is an execution manual for the validation sprint.

It is not a strategy document.
It is not a product plan.
It does not authorize product code, UI redesign, automation, auth, payments, CRM sync, or new SaaS features.

Chris is trying to prove one thing:

> Will founder-led Franchise / Business Consulting operators share customer conversations, value a diagnostic, pay for a Revenue Action Report, execute the recommendation, and report the outcome?

The operating offer:

> Send 10-20 anonymized customer conversations. Get a Free Revenue Leak Diagnostic showing the top leaks and next actions.

The paid offer:

> Revenue Action Report at KRW 390,000 / $299 as the first price test.

The rule:

> No more building until real customers create evidence.

---

## 1. Daily Founder Routine

### Morning

Time block: 90-120 minutes.

Do these in order:

1. Open the outreach tracker.
2. Check yesterday's open loops: replies, promised data, diagnostics due, review calls, paid offers, outcome follow-ups.
3. Pick today's prospect batch.
4. Personalize each message with one specific reason the prospect fits.
5. Send outreach before noon.
6. Update every row immediately after sending.

Morning output:

- 10 high-quality outreach messages sent on normal days.
- 20 messages sent only on heavy sprint days when the list is already prepared.
- All replies from the previous day answered before new research begins.

Morning rule:

> Do not research endlessly. If the prospect fits, send the ask.

### Afternoon

Time block: 2-4 hours.

Do these in order:

1. Run interviews or review calls.
2. Ask for 10-20 anonymized notes during every serious conversation.
3. Chase promised data with simple examples of acceptable snippets.
4. Create or deliver Free Revenue Leak Diagnostics.
5. Make paid Revenue Action Report offers only after the prospect sees a useful diagnostic.
6. Ask every diagnostic recipient to choose one action they would actually execute.

Afternoon output:

- Calls completed.
- Data received or refusal reason recorded.
- Diagnostics delivered.
- Paid offers made when fit is strong.

Afternoon rule:

> Every conversation must end with a concrete next step: send data, book review call, test one action, buy report, or close no-fit.

### Evening

Time block: 45-60 minutes.

Do these in order:

1. Update the founder dashboard.
2. Update the evidence log for every interview, reply, refusal, diagnostic, paid offer, and outcome.
3. Write the strongest quote or objection from the day.
4. Prepare tomorrow's first 10 prospects.
5. Schedule follow-ups.
6. Identify one thing to improve in tomorrow's outreach or diagnostic delivery.

Evening output:

- Tracker clean.
- Follow-up dates set.
- Evidence log updated.
- Tomorrow morning is ready before the day starts.

Evening rule:

> If the tracker is not updated, the day is not finished.

## 2. 50 Prospect Outreach Plan

### Who To Contact First

Contact Franchise / Business Consulting operators before any other vertical.

Priority A:

- Founder-led franchise consultants.
- Business opportunity consultants.
- Startup or self-employment advisors.
- Franchise matching consultants.
- Small consulting teams with visible inbound content and consultation calls.
- Operators who publish content about franchise cost, payback, risk, location, brand comparison, or business ownership.

Priority B:

- Agencies or consultants serving franchise brands.
- Consultants who help business buyers compare opportunities.
- Advisors who handle recurring inquiry or consultation notes.

Priority C:

- Clinics, wedding vendors, home remodeling, hotels, or other adjacent verticals.
- Use these only if the first 50 Franchise / Business Consulting prospects cannot be built or the market rejects the offer with clear evidence.

Do not contact first:

- Enterprise platforms.
- Generic marketing agencies with no high-ticket lead flow.
- Businesses with no written customer language.
- Operators with no recurring inquiries.
- Prospects who only want software demos but will not share data.

### Where To Find Them

Use manual research. Do not wait for automation.

Sources:

- Chris's direct network and warm introductions.
- LinkedIn search for franchise consultant, business consultant, franchise advisor, business opportunity consultant.
- Naver Blog and Google search for franchise startup consulting, franchise cost consulting, business acquisition consulting, self-employment consulting.
- YouTube channels that discuss franchise startup, payback period, investment amount, brand comparison, and location risk.
- Franchise directories and consulting firm websites.
- Blog authors with contact forms or consultation CTAs.
- Consultants posting case studies, FAQ content, or lead magnets.
- Existing content comments where operators discuss lead quality, buyer hesitation, or consultation friction.

For each prospect, capture the reason they fit:

- visible consultation CTA,
- talks about investment or payback,
- publishes franchise/business ownership content,
- appears founder-led,
- likely handles inbound questions,
- has a high-value advisory offer,
- has enough public language to personalize outreach.

### How To Prioritize

Score each prospect from 1 to 5.

5 = contact first:

- Franchise / Business Consulting only.
- Founder or decision maker reachable.
- Publishes content that attracts inbound inquiries.
- Likely has lead notes or consultation snippets.
- Clear high-ticket advisory or commission-driven business.
- Repeated objections are visible in public content.

4 = strong:

- Right market and likely pain, but decision maker access is weaker.

3 = possible:

- Adjacent consulting or agency, but lead workflow is unclear.

2 = weak:

- Interesting business, but no clear recurring lead flow.

1 = skip:

- No written customer conversations, no decision maker, no obvious pain.

Contact order:

1. Warm 5s.
2. Cold 5s with clear personalization.
3. Warm 4s.
4. Cold 4s.
5. Stop before spending time on 3s unless the first pool is too small.

### How Many Per Day

Normal cadence:

- 10 high-quality outreach messages per day.
- 5 data-chase or follow-up messages per day.
- 1-3 calls or review conversations per day.

First 50 sprint cadence:

- Day 1: build all 50, personalize the first 20, send 10-20.
- Day 2: send the next 15-20, reply manually to all responses.
- Day 3: send the final 10-15, chase promised data.
- Day 4-5: no new spray. Follow up, interview, collect data, and deliver diagnostics.

If Chris insists on contacting all 50 tomorrow:

- Send in 5 batches of 10.
- Personalize each batch.
- Update the tracker after each batch.
- Reserve the afternoon for replies and data requests.
- Do not send all 50 if the tracker cannot be maintained.

### Expected Response Rate

Cold outreach expectation:

- 8-15% reply rate from a relevant list.
- 4-8 replies from 50 cold prospects.
- 2-4 serious conversations or data promises.
- 1-3 usable data submissions.

Warm or semi-warm expectation:

- 20-35% reply rate.
- 10-17 replies from 50 warm or semi-warm prospects.
- 5-10 serious conversations or data promises.
- 3-6 usable data submissions.

Strong 14-day signal:

- 50 outreach attempts.
- 10 real conversations or substantive replies.
- 5 usable data submissions.
- 3 free diagnostics delivered.
- 2 paid report offers seriously considered.
- 1 paid report or strong written commitment.
- 2 actions executed.
- 1 outcome recorded.

Bad signal:

- Fewer than 3 replies from 50 qualified prospects.
- Fewer than 2 serious conversations.
- No usable data submitted.
- Prospects understand the offer but do not care.

### When To Follow Up

Cold outreach:

- Day 0: first message.
- Day 2: short relevance check.
- Day 6: example-based follow-up.
- Day 10-14: final close.

Warm intro:

- Day 0: intro message.
- Day 1-2: gentle follow-up if no reply.
- Day 5: final check.

Data promised:

- Same day: send examples of acceptable anonymized notes.
- Day 1: remind with "5 snippets is enough to start."
- Day 3: ask if data sharing is the blocker.
- Day 7: close or move to nurture.

Diagnostic delivered:

- Same day: ask for a 20-minute review call.
- Day 1: ask which action they would actually use.
- Day 4: make or repeat paid report offer if there is strong fit.

Paid offer:

- Same day: send offer summary and price.
- Day 2: answer objections.
- Day 7: final decision request.

Outcome follow-up:

- 7 days after action executed.
- 14 days after content or script action.
- Close if they will not report what happened.

### When To Stop

Stop outreach to a prospect when:

- They say no.
- They have no recurring lead flow.
- They refuse to share even anonymized data.
- They only want general advice.
- They want guaranteed revenue.
- They say ChatGPT is enough and will not test the diagnostic.
- They miss two scheduled calls without explanation.
- They promise data twice and never send it.
- They cannot name one action they would execute.

Move to nurture when:

- Pain is real, but timing is bad.
- They need internal approval for data sharing.
- They ask to revisit after a campaign, event, or sales cycle.

## 4. Pilot Workflow

### Stage 1: First Contact

Goal:

- Get a reply or a data-sharing conversation.

Chris sends:

> I am validating a Free Revenue Leak Diagnostic for franchise and business consulting teams. If you send 10-20 anonymized customer inquiries, consultation notes, or follow-up notes, I will identify the top revenue leaks and the next actions to test. No names or private details needed. Worth trying with a few notes?

Exit criteria:

- Prospect replies.
- Prospect books a call.
- Prospect sends data.
- Prospect declines.

Record:

- Reply.
- Confusion.
- Data concern.
- Pain statement.
- ChatGPT mention.

### Stage 2: Data Received

Goal:

- Confirm there is enough signal to create a diagnostic.

Chris checks:

- Are names, phone numbers, emails, and private data removed?
- Are there 10-20 usable snippets?
- Do the notes include objections, hesitation, follow-up, or lost-lead context?
- Can at least 3 revenue leaks be identified?

If data is weak:

- Tell the prospect exactly what is missing.
- Ask for 5 better snippets.
- Do not fake confidence.

Exit criteria:

- Usable data accepted.
- More data requested.
- Prospect closed because data is unusable.

### Stage 3: Free Revenue Leak Diagnostic

Goal:

- Deliver a useful but incomplete artifact that proves value.

Diagnostic includes:

- Top 3 revenue leaks.
- Repeated objections.
- 3 follow-up or action opportunities.
- 1 sample follow-up message.
- 1 recommended action to test in the next 7 days.
- A short note explaining what the paid report would add.

Do not include:

- A full content calendar.
- A full script library.
- Custom consulting beyond the data.
- Guaranteed revenue claims.
- Product roadmap language.

Exit criteria:

- Diagnostic sent.
- Review call requested.
- One action proposed.

### Stage 4: Review Call

Goal:

- Learn whether the diagnostic creates action and paid intent.

20-minute agenda:

1. Confirm the data source.
2. Walk through the top 3 leaks.
3. Ask which finding surprised them.
4. Ask which action they would actually use.
5. Ask what one recovered consultation is worth.
6. Offer the paid Revenue Action Report if fit is strong.
7. Ask for permission to follow up on the action outcome.

Do not ask:

- Do you like this?
- Would you use software like this?
- What features do you want?

Ask:

- What would you send this week?
- What would make this report worth paying for?
- What would stop you from acting?

Exit criteria:

- Action chosen.
- Paid offer accepted, rejected, or deferred.
- Outcome follow-up scheduled.

### Stage 5: Paid Revenue Action Report

Goal:

- Convert value into payment and deeper learning.

Offer:

> I can turn this into a paid Revenue Action Report with more data reviewed, stronger recommendations, follow-up messages, content ideas, script improvements, and a 7-day action plan.

Price test:

- KRW 390,000 / $299.

Paid report includes:

- Strongest revenue leaks.
- Leads or lead types to rescue.
- Follow-up message library.
- Content ideas tied to real objections.
- Sales script improvements.
- 7-day action plan.
- Outcome tracking plan.

Exit criteria:

- Paid.
- Deferred with specific reason.
- Lost with clear objection.

### Stage 6: Outcome Follow-Up

Goal:

- Prove whether recommendations turn into behavior and results.

Follow up:

- 7 days after a follow-up message or script action.
- 14 days after a content action.

Ask:

- Did you execute the action?
- What exactly did you send, publish, or say?
- Did the lead reply?
- Was a call booked?
- Was a deal won or moved forward?
- Was it ignored?
- What changed your thinking?

Record:

- Action executed.
- Outcome.
- Evidence quality.
- Customer quote.
- Next recommendation.

Exit criteria:

- Outcome recorded.
- No outcome but clear learning.
- Customer refuses to report outcomes.

### Stage 7: Referral Request

Goal:

- Turn proof into distribution.

Ask only after:

- They executed an action.
- They received a reply, booking, won deal, or meaningful learning.
- They clearly found the diagnostic/report useful.

Referral ask:

> Is there one other franchise or business consulting operator who has the same stale-lead or follow-up problem? A simple intro would help me test whether this pattern repeats.

Exit criteria:

- Referral received.
- Referral declined.
- Case study or testimonial requested.

## 6. Decision Rules

### Continue

Continue the experiment when:

- Replies show prospects understand the problem.
- Data Received keeps increasing.
- Diagnostics Delivered produce concrete actions.
- Paid Reports start converting.
- Actions Executed are real, not theoretical.
- Outcomes Recorded show replies, bookings, wins, losses, ignored actions, or useful learning.
- Referrals appear after value is delivered.

Minimum 14-day continue signal:

- 50 Outreach Sent.
- 5+ Replies.
- 3+ Data Received.
- 3 Diagnostics Delivered.
- 1 Paid Report or strong written commitment.
- 1+ Actions Executed.
- 1 Outcome Recorded or scheduled outcome follow-up with a real action already executed.

Strong 90-day continue signal:

- 8+ Data Received.
- 10 Diagnostics Delivered.
- 2+ Paid Reports.
- 3+ Actions Executed.
- 2+ Outcomes Recorded.
- 1+ Referral, setup, or recurring refresh signal.

### Pivot

Pivot when one part works but the current model does not.

Pivot the data request if:

- Prospects feel pain but will not share notes.
- Privacy concerns repeat.
- They need a guided anonymization call.

Pivot the offer if:

- Diagnostics are liked but paid reports do not sell.
- Prospects want a live action session more than a report.
- They only value follow-up messages and ignore content/script suggestions.

Pivot the delivery model if:

- Reports sell but customers do not execute.
- Customers need done-with-you execution.
- The report is too passive.

Pivot the target market if:

- Franchise / Business Consulting produces weak replies, weak data sharing, and no paid intent after 50 qualified prospects.
- Another vertical produces stronger data sharing, paid interest, and action execution with less explanation.

### Change Pricing

Change pricing only after real paid offers.

Lower or repackage price when:

- 5+ paid offers are made.
- Customers say the diagnostic is useful.
- Customers reject the price as too high for a report.
- The objection is price, not lack of pain.

Raise price or move to setup when:

- Buyers accept quickly.
- Delivery takes more than 5 founder hours per report.
- The customer asks for process, templates, team handoff, or repeated use.

Do not change price because of one polite rejection.

### Change Messaging

Change messaging when:

- Prospects ask "What is this?" after reading the outreach.
- Prospects think it is a CRM, review reply tool, or generic AI report.
- Replies mention ChatGPT as enough.
- Fewer than 3 Replies come from the first 30 high-fit messages.

Messaging change hierarchy:

1. Make the data ask more concrete.
2. Make the output more concrete.
3. Remove category language.
4. Use the offer line: "Send 10-20 anonymized notes. Get 3 revenue leaks and 3 follow-up actions."

### Change Target Market

Change target market only with evidence.

Do not change because another market sounds bigger.

Change if:

- The first 50 qualified Franchise / Business Consulting prospects produce weak response, weak data sharing, and weak paid intent.
- Repeated interviews show the pain is not urgent.
- Prospects lack written customer conversations.
- Another vertical produces materially better Data Received, Paid Reports, Actions Executed, or Outcomes Recorded.

### Stop The Experiment

Stop or pause the current direction if:

- 50 qualified Outreach Sent produces fewer than 3 Replies.
- 50 asks produce fewer than 2 usable Data Received.
- 10 Diagnostics Delivered produce 0 Paid Reports or serious paid commitments.
- 5 delivered diagnostics/reports produce 0 Actions Executed.
- 10 Actions Executed produce 0 Outcomes Recorded.
- 50%+ of serious prospects say ChatGPT is enough after seeing the output.
- Every paid delivery requires mostly custom founder judgment by the third paid report.
- No one asks for a second refresh, setup, referral, or recurring help after receiving value.

Stopping is not failure.

It prevents building a company around a belief the market rejected.

## 7. Weekly Review Meeting

Run this every Friday.

Duration:

- 45 minutes.

Inputs:

- Founder Dashboard.
- Outreach Tracker.
- Evidence Log.
- Diagnostics delivered that week.
- Paid offer notes.
- Outcome follow-up notes.

Agenda:

1. Update the dashboard.
2. Review every active prospect with a next step.
3. Read the evidence log aloud.
4. Answer the six weekly questions.
5. Decide what stops next week.
6. Decide what doubles down next week.
7. Pick the first 10 prospects or follow-ups for Monday.

Every Friday answer:

### What did we learn?

Write behavior, not opinion.

Good:

- "Three prospects shared notes only after seeing anonymization examples."

Bad:

- "People like the idea."

### What assumption changed?

Tie it to a named assumption.

Examples:

- Data sharing is easier than expected.
- Paid report value is unclear.
- Follow-up messages matter more than content ideas.
- Franchise consultants already use ChatGPT but still want structured evidence.

### What evidence increased?

Use dashboard numbers and proof.

Examples:

- Data Received increased from 1 to 4.
- Paid Reports increased from 0 to 1.
- Actions Executed increased from 0 to 2.

### What surprised us?

Capture the non-obvious pattern.

Examples:

- They care more about spouse/family approval scripts than payback content.
- They have notes, but not in one place.
- They want a review call more than a written report.

### What should stop?

Stop anything that is not producing evidence.

Examples:

- Stop pitching "Revenue Action Platform" in first-touch copy.
- Stop contacting low-fit agencies.
- Stop including content ideas if no one uses them.

### What should double down?

Double down only on evidence-backed pull.

Examples:

- More warm intros.
- More anonymized data examples.
- More follow-up-message-first diagnostics.
- More prospects with visible consultation content.

Friday output:

- One sentence learning.
- One change for next week.
- One thing to stop.
- One thing to double down.
- Monday's first action.

## 10. Founder Principles

Never build because it sounds cool.

Only build because customers repeatedly ask.

Never confuse opinions with evidence.

Every feature must eliminate uncertainty.

Every sprint must produce learning.

- Sell recovered revenue clarity before selling software.
- Keep the first customer narrow.
- Ask for data before asking for product opinions.
- Ask for payment before assuming willingness to pay.
- Ask for action before assuming value.
